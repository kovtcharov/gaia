## 0. How to read this report

Findings are ranked 🔴 Critical → 🟡 Important → 🟢 Minor per `REVIEW.md`: 🔴 = security, data loss, breaking API, or a bug that fires in normal use; 🟡 = a real bug on an edge path, missing tests for real logic, a convention violation, or a doc that contradicts code; 🟢 = minor. Lint/format/copyright were skipped per `REVIEW.md`. 🔒 marks security findings.

**Verification standard.** Nothing in Sections 2–4 is speculation: each item was confirmed against source at this commit by a first-pass reviewer and then independently re-derived by a second-pass verifier; {{PROBES}} of the critical/important findings were additionally reproduced by executing a probe (marked *probe*), four more against live systems or the installed binary. Claims that could not be confirmed are in Appendix D, labelled as hypotheses. Finding IDs (`C1…`, `I1…`) are stable references for this report; Appendix E maps them to the IDs used in the raw and verification files.

## 0.1 Scope and method

Eight parallel first-pass reviewers each owned one dimension, read their scope fully (large files line-by-line), ran the relevant unit tests where the dimension had any (docs and the Workers/website used live probes and static checks instead), and checked `gh issue list` for existing tracking. A coordinator de-duplicated across reviewers and resolved discrepancies. Four second-pass verifiers then re-derived every finding from source, re-ran probes, and an editorial critic recounted every number and read the highest-risk files the first pass had skipped — adding 15 findings and correcting ~40 claims (Appendix A). Raw per-dimension reports (≈3,600 lines, with quoted evidence) and verification logs (≈5,000 lines) are in `.review/` next to this file.
